---
title: 'Mobile access'
description: 'Use your InboxJarvis deployment from a mobile browser'
icon: 'mobile-screen'
---

InboxJarvis does not currently have its own App Store or Google Play release. Use the web app through a browser connected to your deployment.

`http://localhost:3000` is local to the computer running the development server. To use another device, configure a reachable deployment URL and matching OAuth callbacks.

For setup, see [Quick Start](/hosting/quick-start). For mail and assistant workflows, see [Getting Started](/essentials/getting-started).
